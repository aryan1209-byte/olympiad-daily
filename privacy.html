Privacy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version

Olympiad Daily has no accounts and does not collect your name, email or answers on its own servers. You can use the site without giving any personal information.

Your progress

Your streak, score and today's answer are saved in your own browser (using "local storage"). This information never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data will reset it.

The mock tests early list

If you choose to join the early list, you'll fill in a form hosted by Google Forms, and your email is used only to tell you when mock tests launch. Google handles that form under its own privacy policy. You can ask to be removed at any time by emailing us.

Fonts

The site loads its font from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to download it.

Students under 18

Many visitors are school students. If you're under 18, please check with a parent or guardian before joining the early list.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Questions? Use the Contact link at the bottom of the home page.
